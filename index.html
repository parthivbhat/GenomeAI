EVALUATION-BIAS OBSERVATION

Performance changes under stricter evaluation protocols

The classical models show reduced ROC-AUC under gene-disjoint and temporal evaluation compared with the random split. This demonstrates that measured performance is sensitive to the evaluation protocol and motivates evaluation beyond a random train-test split.
